Daily Health Tips

Small habits, big impact

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration supports all bodily functions and keeps your energy levels high.

Sleep Properly

Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Good sleep restores your immune system and improves cognitive performance.

Eat Balanced

Include fruits, vegetables, lean proteins and whole grains in every meal. Limit processed foods and sugar intake.

Exercise Regularly

At least 30 minutes of moderate activity per day strengthens your heart, muscles, and mental health.

Reduce Stress

Practice mindfulness, meditation, or yoga. Chronic stress weakens immunity and disrupts sleep patterns.

Regular Checkups

Schedule annual health screenings. Early detection saves lives — don't skip your routine medical appointments.
