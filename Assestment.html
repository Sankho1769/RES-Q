Priority Emergency Situations

Critical emergency protocols and response guidelines

🩸
CRITICAL

Heavy Bleeding

✓ Do

  • Apply direct firm pressure
  • Elevate the injured limb
  • Use clean cloth or bandage

✗ Don't

  • Remove embedded objects
  • Use dirty material
  • Leave patient alone
💓
CRITICAL

Heart Attack

✓ Do

  • Sit patient upright
  • Give aspirin if available
  • Loosen tight clothing

✗ Don't

  • Let patient walk alone
  • Give food or water
  • Leave patient unattended
🧠
CRITICAL

Stroke

✓ Do

  • Use FAST test immediately
  • Keep patient comfortable
  • Note time of first symptoms

✗ Don't

  • Give food or medication
  • Let patient sleep it off
  • Delay calling ambulance
🚗
HIGH

Accident Trauma

✓ Do

  • Keep patient still
  • Control visible bleeding
  • Monitor consciousness

✗ Don't

  • Move neck or spine
  • Remove helmets hastily
  • Give water to unconscious
🔥
HIGH

Burns

✓ Do

  • Cool with running water 20min
  • Cover with clean dressing
  • Remove jewelry nearby

✗ Don't

  • Use ice or ice water
  • Break blisters
  • Apply creams or butter
🪘
CRITICAL

Breathing Difficulty

✓ Do

  • Help patient sit upright
  • Loosen tight clothing
  • Use inhaler if available

✗ Don't

  • Let patient lie flat
  • Cause further panic
  • Delay calling help
⚡
HIGH

Seizure

✓ Do

  • Clear area of hazards
  • Cushion the head
  • Time the seizure duration

✗ Don't

  • Restrain the person
  • Put anything in mouth
  • Give water during seizure
🤰
CRITICAL

Pregnancy Emergency

✓ Do

  • Keep patient calm and still
  • Lie on left side if possible
  • Note contraction frequency

✗ Don't

  • Delay calling ambulance
  • Give medications without advice
  • Cause additional stress
🌡
MODERATE

High Fever

✓ Do

  • Apply cool wet cloths
  • Keep patient hydrated
  • Remove excess clothing

✗ Don't

  • Use very cold water
  • Over-bundle patient
  • Ignore temperature above 39°C
